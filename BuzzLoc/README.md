## Problem Statement and Contextualization
Georgia Tech's campus is roughly 400 acres in size, with approximately 15 million square feet of building space across more than 160 buildings. With this amount of space, its traversal becomes a question for the student. 

From most residence halls on campus, students can expect a 15 to 20 minute walk; the alternative: ride one of the campus designated Stinger buses. However, there exists a caveat to this convenience. The buses do not have a set schedule, and therefore arrive at irregular times. Not to mention, without access to a Wi-Fi enabled device, there is no way of getting a bus's location and arrival time.

## Solution statement
Buzz Loc's objective is to make Stinger bus locations accessible offline for students, staff, and visitors of Georgia Tech.
